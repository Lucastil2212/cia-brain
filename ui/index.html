CIA Brain

A library-style finding aid over public agency archives, FOIA reading rooms, and live multimodal open sources — with hybrid search and provenance on every result.

Knowledge graph

Interactive entity neighborhoods. Edges are inferred from source passages — candidates for research, not established facts.

Drag nodes · scroll to zoom · click a node for evidence

Multimodal gallery

Browse image, geospatial, telemetry, and media-reference holdings from live feeds and archives.

Collections

Curated public archives and polling feeds. Status reflects local ingestion, not upstream availability.

Reading room

Ask a local agent to plan, retrieve, and synthesize from catalog evidence. Citations stay grounded in retrieved sources.

Holdings

Paginated inventory of normalized documents in the lake.
